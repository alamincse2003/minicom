1. 30-day money back guarantee

Not completely happy? You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price.

2. Return conditions

  • Items must be unused, in their original condition and, where possible, in the original packaging.
  • Assembled furniture must be disassembled and packed safely for return.
  • Proof of purchase (order number) is required.

3. Items that cannot be returned

  • Custom-made or made-to-order furniture.
  • Items marked as final sale.
  • Gift cards.

4. Damaged or faulty items

Please check your order on delivery. If anything arrives damaged or faulty, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we will arrange a free repair, replacement or refund.

5. Exchanges

Want a different colour or size? Return the original item and place a new order, or contact us and we will help arrange the exchange.

6. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping costs are not refundable unless the item was damaged or faulty.

7. How to start a return

Email support@domain.com, call (012)-345-67890, or use our contact form. Include your order number and the items you want to return, and we will send you return instructions.
