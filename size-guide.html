How to measure your space

  • Measure the room and mark where the furniture will go with painter’s tape.
  • Leave at least 75–90 cm walkways around beds and tables.
  • Check door frames, stairs and lifts on the delivery route: the box must fit through.
  • For coffee tables, keep about 40–45 cm between the table and the sofa.
