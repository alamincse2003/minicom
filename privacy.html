1. Information we collect

When you browse, register or place an order we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number.
  • Delivery and billing addresses.
  • Order history and the products you save to your wishlist.
  • Technical data such as your browser, device and pages visited.

Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send newsletters and offers, only if you have agreed to receive them.
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and your browser’s local storage to remember your cart, wishlist and account sign-in on this device. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; your cart and wishlist will then be emptied.

4. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted partners who help us run the store, such as payment processors, delivery companies and email providers, and only as far as needed for them to do their job.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in every email.

6. Data retention

We keep order information for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes, and other personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

7. Children

Our store is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact
